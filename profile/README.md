# Innova Integra Limited

We are an AI research and development company. We carry out applied AI research, consult and provide expertise on AI to our clients, and offer full planning and execution services for AI projects as contractors.

We focus on novel and specialist applications of artificial intelligence that have measurable real-life benefits and that are easy to use and understand.

## What We Do

- **Practical Machine Learning** — applying and customising state-of-the-art machine learning and LLM-driven approaches across video analytics, natural language processing, data stream mining, reinforcement learning, hybrid learning, hardware acceleration, explainability, and security/privacy-preserving ML.
- **Agent Modelling & Predictive Simulations** — modelling and simulating agent behaviour over long time periods, including reinforcement learning and general predictive simulation methods.
- **Graph Data Models & Algorithms** — semantic data representation, social network analytics, and efficient graph algorithms, including graph representations of real-world social environments.
- **Combinatorial Optimisation** — mathematically modelling and solving combinatorial optimisation problems using both classical and novel approaches.
- **Social Systems** — collective awareness platforms, collaboration platforms, digital social innovation, social robotics, and decision-support systems for groups of users and human-machine collaboration.
- **Information Visualisation** — techniques for visualising statistical data and research system results.
- **Software & Systems Development** — designing and building systems from prototype to commercial release using modern service-oriented architectures, container-based deployment, and modern web/app interfaces.
- **Project Management & Delivery** — technical project planning, agile software delivery, requirements engineering, user-system interaction design, and privacy/ethics-by-design (including GDPR compliance).

## Current Projects

We are active in several collaborative research initiatives, including:

- **iDT4GDC** — optimising resource usage in data centres for better energy use and efficiency.
- **ULTIMATE** — an EU Horizon Europe project, joined as a full partner.
- **AI4SWEng** — a Horizon Europe project applying AI across the software development lifecycle.

New projects starting soon include **uPQComing** (post-quantum cryptography), **Truthaware** (social media analytics), and **Build-Bone** (bone health).
